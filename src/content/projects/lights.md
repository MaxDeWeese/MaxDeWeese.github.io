## Overview

A custom-built wave projector designed to produce dynamic, lighting effects through mechanical motion and optical distortion. 

Tech used:
- CAD, 3D Printing, Mechanical Design, Optics
- Raspberry Pi, Electronics Assembly, LED Lighting, DC Motors

## Mechanical Overview 

### Drive and Optical System  

A dc motor powers the turret through a bevel gear setup. A piece of wave acrylic is placed within the turret ~ 1 cm above a ring of multicolored LEDs.
